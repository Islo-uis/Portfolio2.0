Exhibit 01 · 2024 · Social Media Design

Sport Court Ontario

Built around the conviction that sport surfaces shouldn't need corporate language to earn trust. The system creates consistency without sacrificing warmth.

Ongoing social media content system for a professional court surface company in Canada. Developed visual frameworks, content templates, writing guidelines, and a scheduling approach that keeps the brand active without losing coherence.

Social Media Brand Systems Content Design Canva

Role: Social Media Manager & Content Designer

Exhibit 02 · 2024 · Web Design & Development

Personal Portfolio

The challenge of designing for yourself is that there is no brief you can hide behind. Every decision is a statement.

Single-file portfolio site designed and developed as an ongoing project. Built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, prioritizing fast load, clean architecture, and a visual identity that doesn't feel borrowed.

Exhibit 03 · 2025 · Event Design

ABECON 2025

An event conference platform that needed to feel official without feeling bureaucratic. The visual direction had to carry authority without closing the door on younger participants.

Visual design and digital materials for ABECON 2025. Developed brand assets, layouts, and digital communication materials for the conference environment.

Web and digital work for the Philippine Council for Agriculture, Aquatic and Natural Resources Research and Development. Focus on improving the usability and visual presentation of institutional digital materials.

I work at the intersection of web development, design, and content — which is less a niche than it sounds. Most digital work requires all three at once, and I've found that being comfortable across them makes the individual parts stronger.

My background is in front-end development and UI/UX design, but it extends into graphic design, publication layout, short-form video, and content systems. I'm currently completing a BS in Information Technology with a major in Information Security at the University of Southeastern Philippines.

I'm drawn to projects where the quality of thinking is visible in the final output. I work carefully, question assumptions, and try to make things that feel considered rather than assembled.
